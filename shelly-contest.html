THE PROJECT

What is SolarDiverter LITE?

SolarDiverter LITE is firmware for the ESP32 microcontroller that intelligently diverts excess solar energy to resistive loads — typically water heaters, immersion heaters, or thermosiphons — using PWM modulation.

Instead of injecting unused solar power back into the grid at low feed-in tariffs, SolarDiverter modulates a TRIAC-controlled load in real time to absorb exactly the amount of surplus being produced.

The key ingredient is accurate energy sensing — and that's where Shelly comes in.

SHELLY INTEGRATION

Three Shelly roles — one smart system

SolarDiverter LITE supports up to three independent Shelly devices, each playing a distinct role in the energy measurement chain.

Role 1 — Grid / Surplus Source
🔌 Grid Meter

Measures the power imported or exported at the main electrical panel. SolarDiverter reads grid_power (positive = import, negative = export) and uses it as the primary input to compute how much solar surplus is available.

When grid power is negative (exporting to grid), the firmware activates PWM proportionally to absorb that surplus in the load.

Shelly EM Shelly 3EM Shelly EM Gen3

Supports Gen1 (/status) and Gen2/Gen3 (/rpc/Shelly.GetStatus) auto-detected.

Role 2 — PV Production (optional)
☀️ PV Meter

A separate Shelly installed at the PV inverter output measures raw solar production. This provides the pv_power reading, enabling accurate energy flow display: PV → House → Diverter → Grid.

Without a dedicated PV meter, SolarDiverter estimates PV production from grid and load data. With it, the UI shows the full picture.

Shelly EM Shelly 1PM Any Gen1/2/3

Configured as a secondary data source. Polled independently from the grid meter.

Role 3 — Load Meter (new in v1.1.3)
♨️ Load Meter

A Shelly Plug or 1PM placed in series with the resistive load (water heater, immersion heater) measures the real power actually consumed — instead of relying on the PWM duty-cycle estimate.

This closes the feedback loop: the ESP32 can see in real time how many watts are reaching the load, compensating for TRIAC non-linearity, load resistance variation, and voltage fluctuations.

When active, the WebUI shows a REAL badge in the energy flow and a live power card in the configuration tab.

LOAD METER — DEEP DIVE

How the Load Meter works

PWM-controlled TRIACs are inherently non-linear. A 50% duty cycle does not deliver exactly 50% of rated power — it depends on load resistance, mains voltage, and TRIAC characteristics. The Load Meter solves this:

// Simplified logic in system_core.cpp if (cfg.load_meter_enabled && shellyReachable) { state.load_power_from_meter = true; state.load_power = shellyPlugin.power_w; // real watts } else { state.load_power_from_meter = false; state.load_power = rated_load_w * (pwm / 100.0f); // estimate }

The real reading is exposed via /api/status as load_power and load_power_from_meter: true. The WebUI shows a green REAL badge in the energy flow diagram whenever the meter is active and reachable.

Accurate surplus tracking — watt by watt.
The system always knows exactly how much power the load is consuming, not just an estimate.
HARDWARE SETUP

Typical installation

1. Shelly EM at Main Panel

Install current clamps on the grid feed. Shelly EM connects to your WiFi and exposes real-time import/export power via HTTP JSON.

2. ESP32 + TRIAC board

Flash SolarDiverter LITE, configure WiFi and Shelly IP. The ESP32 reads grid power every 2–5 seconds and adjusts PWM output.

3. Shelly Plug on the Load

Plug the Shelly Plug between the TRIAC output and the water heater. Enable "Load Meter" in the WebUI and enter the Shelly's IP.

4. Ready — surplus goes to hot water

Solar surplus is absorbed by the water heater instead of being exported. The WebUI shows real power, temperature, and energy flow live.
